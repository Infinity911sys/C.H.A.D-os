About Austin Enterprise

Austin Enterprise Inc is a technology and research organization founded and directed by Chad Alan Austin, based in Phoenix, Arizona, United States.

Founded on principles of Conscious, Honest, Accountable, and Disciplined (C.H.A.D.) design, Austin Enterprise develops operating systems and research frameworks that advance human potential and institutional integrity.

Our mission encompasses:

  • 🖥️ Operating Systems: C.H.A.D. OS and InfinityOS ecosystems
  • 📖 Publishing: C.H.A.D. OS Press - The Green World, The Educated World
  • 🎵 Music: C.H.A.D. Music Productions distribution
  • 🔬 Research: C.H.A.D. Research Institute - Evidence-based behavioral systems
  • 📱 Mobile: Android applications on Google Play Store
